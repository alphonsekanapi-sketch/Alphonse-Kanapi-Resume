About me

Connecting people, systems, and learning.

I am a Learning Experience Lead who shapes learning strategy into practical programs, strengthens HR technology ecosystems, and builds scalable people operations across global teams.

My work begins with learning strategy, where I turn capability needs into clear development priorities, then extends into HR technology through the design and administration of LMS, LXP, HRIS, and compensation systems. From there, I apply data governance to create cleaner, more reliable information flows, and use process improvement to simplify workflows and improve consistency.

I have supported distributed teams across the United States, Canada, Mexico, Nicaragua, South Africa, and the Philippines, giving me strong grounding in global operations. Across each initiative, my focus stays on creating a better employee experience by making systems, services, and learning journeys easier to understand, easier to use, and more meaningful in day to day work.
