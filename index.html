About me

Connecting people, systems, and learning.

I am a Learning Experience Lead working across learning, HR technology, and global people operations. I have supported teams across the United States, Nicaragua, Canada, Mexico, South Africa, and the Philippines, helping build experiences and systems that are practical, scalable, and easier to use.

My work sits at the intersection of learning strategy, HRIS, LMS and LXP management, data governance, and process improvement. I focus on turning complex processes into clear workflows and creating learning experiences that solve real performance needs.
